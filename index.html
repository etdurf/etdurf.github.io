Dibs

Call it before you forget it.

Some things surface in the middle of an ordinary day. The car payment, the pickup on Thursday. You can't settle them alone, and you don't want to carry them until you can. Dibs lets you toss it up so it leaves your head and lands somewhere you'll both see it. Once someone calls dibs, it's handled, and you can both stop holding it.

  • Type it the way you'd say it"Pick up the car Friday at 7pm" becomes a task with a due date. Try it in the phone.
  • Discussion TimeOne nudge a day, at an hour the couple picks, about whatever is still in the air.
  • The toss and the catchI wrote the animations into the spec as non-negotiable. Handing something off should feel like a relief.

A couples app, in progress. Designed in Claude Design as five screens of sky and air, then built with Expo, React Native and Supabase realtime.

The reading challenge

239 chapters, September 10 to Christmas.

My family set out to read the Book of Mormon together before December 25. The app splits every chapter evenly across 104 days and leaves three catch-up days before Christmas, so nobody finishes behind.

  • Encouragement, never a rankingThe family tab shows bars and streaks in alphabetical order. Nobody is first or last.
  • Takeaways are anonymousYou write one thought a day and it joins the family's list without your name on it.

In use this fall. React, TypeScript, Firebase Auth and Firestore.
